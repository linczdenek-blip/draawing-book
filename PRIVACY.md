# DoodlePop Privacy Policy

*Last updated: June 2026*

DoodlePop is a coloring app for children. It is designed **privacy-first** and
intended to comply with COPPA (US) and GDPR-K (EU) requirements for apps
directed at children.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** DoodlePop has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no ads and
no third-party SDKs.

## Where data lives

All data — drawings, kid profile names/ages/avatars, and converted photos —
is stored **only on the device**, in the app's local SwiftData store. Deleting
the app deletes all of it.

## Photos

The "Photo magic" feature uses the system photo picker (the app sees only the
single photo the user explicitly selects) and converts it to a line-art
coloring page **on the device**, using Apple's Core Image and Vision
frameworks. Photos are never uploaded anywhere.

## Sharing

A drawing can leave the device only through the system share sheet (e.g.
AirDrop, Messages, printing), and only after answering a parental-gate
question that small children cannot pass. Nothing is shared automatically.

## Kid profiles

Profile name, age and avatar emoji are used solely for the on-device greeting
and for organizing each child's drawings. They are not transmitted anywhere.

## Network access

The app makes **no network requests**.
